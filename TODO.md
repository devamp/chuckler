## Prompt types

Every prompt belongs to one segment, shown by the badge in the top right of the prompt:

- **Friends**: created by a user for their friends, or generated by AI for a friend group. Only that circle sees and answers it.
- **Local**: prompts for people within x miles. Comes **after launch** (see Local below).
- **Global**: provided by the app for everyone.

Later, if the app grows, Local and Global prompt slots could be sold to companies as sponsored prompts. These must carry a visible "Sponsored" label.

**Open question:** for a Friends prompt, is the audience the creator's friends list, or a group the creator picks? Friend lists are not mutual circles: my friends cannot necessarily see each other.

## 2. Home tab: swipe voting

A matchup shows two answer cards stacked vertically.

| Gesture | Effect |
|---|---|
| Swipe **right** on a card | That answer wins |
| Swipe **left** on a card | Downvote that answer |
| Swipe **up** | Skip (before voting) / next matchup (after voting), like a reels feed |
| Swipe **down** or **back** button | Previous matchup |

- **One action per matchup:** a win or a downvote, never both. A downvote on one answer does not count as a win for the other.
- **Votes are final.** Back shows a previous matchup's results and your vote, but it cannot change the vote. A skipped matchup has no vote yet, so back can still vote on it.
- **After voting, the results appear immediately:** each card's pick percentage ("62% picked this", counting only right swipes) and the comments on the answer you acted on.
- **Comments are per answer**, one thread for the answer's whole lifetime, so they follow it as matchups change through the day and into the Past feed. In voting they're visible only after you vote: a preview of the answer you picked or downvoted appears with the results; tapping a card opens that answer's full thread, so you can read the other answer's comments too. Only voters can comment.
- **A downvote is a dislike** on that answer, the same as disliking it in the Past feed. An answer can be disliked once per user. Dislikes are anonymous: the author sees only the count. Likes store the likers' uids. The list of what you liked or disliked is kept on the phone.
- **Vote cards are anonymous until you vote**: matchups don't name their authors. After your vote (a win or a downvote), both cards show their author's icon and username on the right of the top row.
- **Teaching the gestures:** a one-time overlay the first time someone opens Home ("Swipe right on the winner, left to downvote, up to skip, down to go back"), plus matching buttons under the cards for discoverability and VoiceOver.
- Accept swipes only when they start on the cards, away from the screen edges, so they don't collide with the iOS home and Notification Center gestures.
- Each card shows whether the answer is from a friend, local or global. Not filtered by default; the user can filter to specific types. Friend and Local are relative to the viewer, so the tag is worked out per viewer, not stored on the post.

**Built so far (live in the emulator):** the Vote | Past toggle on Home, the swipe screen (`lib/pages/feedpage/feedpage_vote.dart`, `feedpage_vote_card.dart`), `VotingService`, results and the one-time tutorial. Today's matchups come from `MatchUps` (anonymous until you vote, then both authors show), votes are saved once per user in `Votes` (refused votes are taken back with a message), a downvote leaves an anonymous dislike, and answer comments (`feedpage_comments.dart`) read and write `Posts/{id}/CommentPages`; the preview after voting shows the comment count and latest comment. The caught-up screen can check for new matchups.

## 3. Past responses feed

- The current scrolling Home feed is kept and repurposed to show **past responses**, separate from voting. Reach it from a "Vote | Past" toggle at the top of Home.
- "Past" means prompts whose lifetime has ended (see below).
- Shows each past prompt's winner and final percentages.
- **Comments are the same per-answer threads as in voting**, carried over unchanged.
- Answers show with usernames, strangers' included: voting is over, so the anonymity ends. Read from each prompt's `Results` pages.

## 4. Prompt lifetime and ranking (after the mechanics are settled)

Each prompt has a **lifetime**. At checkpoints during it, the server makes decisions about the field:

- **Cut down the field:** retire answers that do badly.
- **Change matchups:** when an answer is retired for heavy downvotes, re-pair its opponent with a fresh answer, so one bad answer doesn't sink a good one. Extends `createMatchup`, which already pairs unmatched posts.
- **Highlight matchups:** show strong matchups more as the day goes on, so a winner emerges.
- When the lifetime ends, the prompt moves to the Past feed with its winner.

Ranking must use **rates, not raw counts**, or matchups made early in the day always win:
- **No view counting** (too many writes). A matchup's rate is wins ÷ (wins + dislikes).
- Each matchup gets a minimum number of votes before it can be judged (20 is a starting guess; tune it).
- Score by picks, comments and likes per vote, minus dislikes per vote.

All of this runs **server-side** (scheduled Cloud Functions at the checkpoints) so users can't game it.

**Open questions:** how long a lifetime is (and whether Friends prompts differ from Global), where the checkpoints fall, and what each one cuts.

## Local (after launch)

- **Privacy:** needs a location permission and a usage explanation for App Store review. Store only coarse location (a city or low-precision geohash), never exact coordinates.
- **Queries:** "within x miles" in Firestore means geohash range queries plus filtering in the app.
- **Too few users:** Local will often be empty at first. Widen the radius automatically or fall back to Global.
